What it knows

Real clearances

A 2'-0" kitchen platform, 2'-0" deep wardrobes, 3'-0" doors, a 3'-6" passage, a 3'-0" shower. Rooms are sized around what has to fit in them, not the other way round.

Your plot, not a template

Drop a drawing and it thresholds the ink, floods the ground, and traces whatever line encloses the plot — L-shapes, cut corners and all.

Two drawings that agree

The dimensioned plan and the 3D cut-away are the same model. Change anything and both move together, along with the areas and the schedule.

Inside every room

A perspective from the corner of each room with the two near walls taken away — the cut a sectional perspective makes, so you see the bed and the wardrobe rather than the back of a wall.

Said in your words

“Make the kitchen bigger”, “kitchen aur dining badal do”, “500 gaj north facing”. Read on the page itself, so it answers before you have let go of the key.

One sheet to send

Plan, 3D and the full room schedule on a single sheet, stamped essentia — as a JPG, a PDF, or copied straight to the clipboard for a message.
