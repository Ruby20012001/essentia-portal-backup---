Section Two

What the portal is

System philosophy · What it replaces · What it enables that nothing else could

The Group essentia portal is the single operating environment across both businesses. It replaces WhatsApp groups, Excel trackers, fragmented email chains, and manual tracking as the place where every project is managed, every client is known, every document is generated, and every process is enforced. It is not a dashboard for reporting. It is a generation engine (produces finished documents from data), a workflow enforcer (some things cannot happen without prior steps confirmed), an intelligence layer (surfaces patterns, risks, and resource conflicts before they become crises), and a relationship memory (every client interaction, across both businesses, permanently recorded and searchable).

Critical architecture principle: The portal functions as a standalone website-domain tool. It does not depend on any AI provider to operate. The AI layer is modular — Microsoft 365 Copilot is the primary AI engine (plugging into the group's existing Microsoft ecosystem: Teams, Outlook, SharePoint, Entra ID). Anthropic Claude is available as a secondary modular option for specific tasks. If either AI provider is unavailable, the portal continues to function — every module works, every document can be generated from templates, every workflow runs. The AI layer enhances; it does not underpin.

The Core Principle — AI Enhances, Does Not Underpin

The portal works completely without any AI provider active

Every module, every workflow, every enforcement rule, every document generation — all must function from templates and structured data without requiring an active AI connection. AI improves the quality of outputs (better-drafted letters, smarter pattern recognition, more contextual alerts). It does not determine whether the portal functions at all.

This is not an optional design choice — it is a mandatory architectural requirement. If Microsoft 365 Copilot has a service outage, the portal continues. If Anthropic's API is unavailable, the portal continues. No dependency on a single AI provider.

Microsoft 365 Copilot — Primary AI Layer

Why Copilot is the primary choice

The group already runs on Microsoft 365 — Teams (via Microsoft Graph API), Outlook, SharePoint, Entra ID SSO, and OneDrive. Copilot integrates natively with all of these. Staff using Copilot in the portal are using the same AI they can use in Outlook and Teams — one learning curve, one subscription, one data residency standard.

Anthropic Claude — Modular Secondary Option

Claude (claude-sonnet-4-6) remains available as a secondary AI option for specific tasks where its capabilities outperform Copilot — particularly in voice analysis (Voice of Site), long-form document generation from complex multi-source data, and nuanced tone calibration for client communications. The AI layer must be built as a pluggable interface: any AI call in the portal specifies the task type, and the system routes it to the appropriate provider based on configuration. Switching from Copilot to Claude for a specific module requires a configuration change, not a code change.

Recommended implementation: Abstract all AI calls behind a single AIProvider interface. The interface accepts: task type (drafting / analysis / classification / summarisation), input data, and output format. The provider configuration (Copilot or Claude) is set at the system level and can be overridden per module. This is the architecture that makes the portal provider-independent.

The NOC Protocol -- No Objection Certificate After Each Major Activity
  • NOC is issued by the CRM after each major activity is confirmed complete by VisionCAM
  • The NOC confirms: the activity is complete, it has been inspected, the next activity can begin
  • Activities that always require a NOC before the next phase begins: waterproofing (before PCC), underground plumbing (before screeding), HVAC commissioning, electrical DB and earthing, carpentry per room (before stone counters), fire fighting system

  • Disputed NOC: if the contractor disputes that the NOC condition has been met, the matter goes to Bashruddin for field inspection. The portal records the dispute and the resolution.

  • When essentia coordinates third-party vendors (electrical, HVAC, fire-fighting, automation, specialist stone, kitchen, trap doors), a 20% coordination surcharge is added to the vendor's actual cost

  • The coordination charge is disclosed in the BOQ at contractor appointment stage -- never as a surprise

Week 1 -- The Guided Walk

Every new user gets a 5-session guided walkthrough of their specific portal surface. Each session is 10 minutes and covers one core workflow. The walkthrough is interactive: the person performs an action in a test environment, not just watches a video. Sessions unlock one per day. They cannot all be completed on Day 1. This is pacing for learning, not bureaucratic gating. By Day 5 the person has performed each of their 5 core workflows at least once.

Month 1 -- The Standard Revealed

At Day 30, the portal generates a first-month review: what the newcomer was supposed to do in Month 1 (from their KRA), what they actually did (from their portal activity), where the gap is, and who reviews this with them. This is not a performance evaluation. It is an alignment conversation. The newcomer sees their own review before their manager does. They come to the conversation with self-awareness, not with defensiveness.

The Newcomer's North Star -- Per Role, Not Generic
  • CRM TL newcomer sees: 'Your first action today is to read every Family Profile in your project portfolio. Then call each client within 48 hours. Your job is to make each person feel that the relationship has not skipped a beat.' Not generic CRM training. Specific to their first week.
  • Junior CRM newcomer sees: 'Today you shadow your TL. Tomorrow you write a draft daily report for one project before your TL does. On Day 3 you compare yours to theirs. The gap between those two documents is your development path.'
  • Site supervisor newcomer sees: 'Before your first site visit, read the GFC drawings for the room you are supervising. Not to understand them fully -- to know what the finished room is supposed to look like. VisionCAM will ask you to confirm the work matches the drawing. You need to know what the drawing says.'
  • Factory HOD newcomer sees: 'Your first morning is walking the floor with Mabud Rahaman. Not managing. Listening. The factory has a rhythm. You are learning the rhythm before you change anything in it.'
  • EH CA newcomer sees: 'Do not start by learning the products. Start by observing 10 client interactions -- how experienced CAs open, qualify, and close. Write one observation after each interaction. Product knowledge comes in your second week. Client understanding comes in your first.'
  • BD executive newcomer sees: 'Read the last 5 proposals generated in this firm. Not to copy them -- to understand what essentia considers a complete proposal. Then read the conversion history for each: did it convert? Why or why not? This is your education in what works.'
